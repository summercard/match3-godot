# Noto Sans CJK

`NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc` is the regular OpenType collection from the
[official Noto CJK repository](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/tree/main/Sans/OTC).
It contains the Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese
regional faces used by the runtime font registry.
